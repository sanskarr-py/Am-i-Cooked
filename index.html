How It Works

The Cooked Score is computed through a deterministic study velocity model that weighs 5 key variables:

Cooked Score = f(Workload Needed, Available Hours, Baseline Retention)
  • Syllabus Density: Topics are estimated based on curriculum size and baseline study hours needed.
  • Foundation Multiplier: Lacking fundamentals adds a 1.35× to 1.8× multiplier on study time needed.
  • Time Deficit: Calculates whether your daily available hours can physically cover the required curriculum before exam morning.
  • Score Calibration: Normalizes from 0% (total safety) to 100% (academic emergency).
